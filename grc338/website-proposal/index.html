Introduction

Stardust Flower Company

We are Stardust Flower Company, a universal flower delivery company. Whether you are across the galaxy, the next star system over, or down the street, we deliver! Stardust flowers are the freshest flowers for your event thanks to our top of the line suspended animation chambers and wormhole transit system. We cater for any event from the beginning of life to the end and every celebration in between.

Target audience

The target audience for this company is anyone who wants Earth’s flowers for an event. And I mean anyone, even if they are across the universe (as long as they have access to Earth’s internet).

The audience for this website would choose this website and company because they want the top of the line flowers from Earth at their event. Flowers are the perfect decoration for any major or minor event in someone’s life, be it a wedding, funeral, birth, or graduation.
