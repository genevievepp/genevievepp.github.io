We are Stardust Flower Co, a universal flower delivery company. Whether you are across the galaxy, the next star system over, or down the street, we deliver!

We provide the freshest flowers for your event thanks to our top of the line suspended animation chambers and wormhole transit system. We cater for any event from the beginning of life to the end and every celebration in between. With our wide array of flowers, from roses and tulips to mushrooms and succulents, we promise to set your event with the best the Earth has to offer.

From our humble beginnings in San Luis Obispo County, California, United States of America, Earth, Stardust Flower Company has bloomed our business into a success that just keeps growing! We are a human women-led business that is dedicated to sharing our planet’s flowers with the galaxy. From roses and lilies to passion flowers and orchids, we are committed to sharing and preserving our flowers. Many flowers have gone extinct in the wild, the only remaining descendants living in climate-controlled environments. As an Earthen flower company, our mission is to share our planet’s remaining florals with the rest of the galaxy and our Earthen neighbors who also believe in our preservations.
